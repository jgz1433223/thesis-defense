# 答辩讲稿（1-4页）


## Slide 1：标题 + 个人故事（1.5 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 标题页：论文题目、姓名、学号、学院、导师
- 右下角可以放一张钢铁厂/工厂的意象图

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "Good afternoon, professors. My name is Jiang Guanzhou, from HSBC Business School, majoring in Fintech, supervised by Professor Tong Naqiong."
>
> "Before I present my thesis, let me start with a personal story."
>
> "My grandfather ran a steel business. Before 2010, when China's economy was booming, his company grew rapidly — expanding production lines, hiring aggressively, building new facilities. Business was great. He expanded confidently."
>
> "But then the macroeconomy slowed. Overcapacity hit the steel industry hard. Revenue started falling — at first gradually, then sharply."
>
> "Here's the problem: **when revenue dropped, the costs didn't drop with it.** All those employees, those factory leases, those administrative overheads — they were stuck. They couldn't come down fast enough. That rigid cost structure eventually overwhelmed the business."
>
> "That experience stayed with me. It made me ask: **why is it so hard for firms to reduce costs when revenue declines?** And more importantly — **can anything help?** "
>
> "This led me to the academic literature on cost stickiness, and eventually to my research question: **can China's Digital Economy Pilot Zones — a major institutional innovation — provide a solution to this problem?** "


## Slide 2：费用粘性 — 定义与成因（1 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 左侧：不对称曲线图（收入 vs 费用）
- 右侧：两个方块 — Agency Costs | Adjustment Costs
- 下方标注 ABJ (2003) 文献

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "What my grandfather experienced is formalized in the accounting literature as **cost stickiness**. Anderson, Banker, and Janakiraman — the ABJ model — defined it in 2003 as an asymmetric cost behavior: when revenue rises by 10%, expenses increase by about 5%; but when revenue falls by 10%, expenses might only decrease by 2%. **Costs go up like an elevator but come down like an escalator.** "
>
> "From my literature review, I found two core drivers of this phenomenon. First, **agency costs** — managers have incentives to build business empires and maintain perquisites, so they resist cutting resources when revenue declines. Second, **adjustment costs** — firing employees and divesting assets is legally and reputationally expensive."
>
> "Importantly, this isn't just an accounting curiosity. **It's a governance problem** — and it means a purely internal solution may not be enough. You might need an external institutional intervention."


## Slide 3：文献缺口 — 从企业数字化到政策数字化（1.5 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 左侧：现有文献列表（企业数字化转型 ✓，数字金融 ✓）
- 右侧：一个大问号 + "Policy-level digital economy?"
- 箭头从"企业内部"指向"政策层面"，标注"This thesis"

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "After understanding cost stickiness, I went to the literature to see: **what solutions have already been identified?** "
>
> "I found a growing body of research showing that digital transformation — at the firm level — can reduce cost stickiness. Scholars like Wu Wuqing (2022), Wang Chengcai (2024), and Zhang Yong (2023) have confirmed this effect. Similarly, research on digital finance — by Yuan Chun (2025) and others — also finds a suppressing effect."
>
> "But here's what struck me: **almost all existing research focuses on digital development from the firm's own perspective** — a company's own digital transformation, or its access to digital financial services. **What about the policy level?** "
>
> "The National Digital Economy Innovation Development Pilot Zones represent the **government's top-down push for digitalization** — providing infrastructure, encouraging data sharing, reforming institutions. This is a fundamentally different angle: **digital economy development as a national policy, not just a firm-level choice.** "
>
> "I realized: **no study had examined whether this policy-level digital economy initiative could reduce corporate cost stickiness.** That became the core question of my thesis."


## Slide 4：研究设计与数据（1.5 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- DID 2x2 矩阵示意图（Before/After × Treatment/Control）
- 公式行：DID = Treat × After
- 数据清洗流程图（漏斗形：原始数据 → 剔除 → 缩尾 → 19019 obs）

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "To test this question, I used a **Difference-in-Differences** model."
>
> "The **treatment group** is firms registered in the six pilot zones. The **control group** is firms outside these zones. The **policy shock** is 2019 — when the zones were established."
>
> "**Why DID?** Because firm-level digitalization is endogenous — a firm that chooses to digitalize may already perform better. But the pilot zone is an **exogenous policy shock**. Firms didn't choose to be in it. This gives us clean causal identification."
>
> "Now let me walk through my data. I started with all A-share listed firms from 2016 to 2024. I went through a careful cleaning process: excluding financial and insurance firms, removing ST and delisted firms, dropping observations with missing key variables, and winsorizing at the 1% level."
>
> "After this process, I obtained **19,019 firm-year observations**. Data comes from the iFind and CSMAR databases. All regressions were done in **Stata 18.0**."
>
> "I want to emphasize: **this was where I learned the most about empirical research.** The decisions you make in data cleaning directly affect your results. Every exclusion criterion, every winsorization choice — I had to justify each one. It taught me that rigorous research begins long before the regression."

## Slide 5：核心发现 — 结果展示（2 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 平行趋势图（事件研究法折线图 + 95%置信区间）
- 基准回归系数图（点图显示 DID 系数 = 0.110***）
- 不要表格，用图

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "Now let me show you what I found."
>
> "First, the **parallel trend test** — this is crucial for DID validity. As this graph shows, before 2019, there was no systematic difference between treatment and control groups. The coefficients are all statistically insignificant. After the policy shock in 2019, the effect becomes positive and significant. **This confirms the parallel trend assumption holds.** "
>
> "Now the **baseline regression result**. The coefficient of interest — the interaction term DID — is **0.110, significant at the 1% level**. What does this mean? In the ABJ model, a negative coefficient on *ΔRev × Dec* indicates cost stickiness exists. A positive coefficient on the triple interaction *ΔRev × Dec × DID* means **the pilot zone policy significantly weakens this stickiness.** "
>
> "To put it in economic terms: **firms in the pilot zones show much less resistance to cutting SG&A expenses when revenue declines** — about an 11 percentage point reduction in stickiness compared to non-pilot firms."
>
> "The model explains 32.4% of the variation with 19,019 observations, giving us strong statistical power."


## Slide 6：机制 — 三条路径（2.5 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 一个流程图：试验区 → 三条路径（劳动力↑ / 研发↑ / 内控↑）→ 费用粘性↓
- 右侧用三个小型系数图分别展示每条路径的中介效应

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "The headline result is clear: the pilot zones reduce cost stickiness. But **I wanted to understand how**. This is where I think my thesis makes its main contribution — opening the black box of transmission mechanisms."
>
> "I identified and tested **three channels**."
>
> "**Channel 1: Labor Structure.** The policy significantly increases the proportion of highly educated employees — those with bachelor's degrees or higher. Why does this matter? Highly educated workers have greater cognitive flexibility. They can be reassigned across departments during downturns instead of being laid off, which reduces the adjustment costs that cause stickiness. The mediation effect is statistically significant at the 1% level."
>
> "**Channel 2: R&D Investment.** The pilot zones provide tax incentives and subsidies that encourage firms to invest more in R&D. This funds digital transformation — cloud systems, intelligent supply chains, automated processes. These technologies convert rigid fixed costs into variable costs. Instead of owning servers and warehouses, firms can rent cloud services and use smart logistics. When revenue drops, these variable costs can be cut quickly. Again, the mediation effect is significant at 1%."
>
> "**Channel 3: Agency Costs.** This is perhaps the most interesting channel. The pilot zones push firms to adopt digital internal control systems — ERPs, financial shared service centers, blockchain-based audit trails. These technologies **penetrate through traditional organizational hierarchies** and make it much harder for managers to hide redundant expenses or maintain perquisite consumption. The data shows a significant improvement in internal control quality, which then reduces cost stickiness. **The coefficient is 0.111, significant at 1%.** "
>
> "In summary: the policy works through **people** (better labor), **technology** (more R&D), and **governance** (stronger controls). These three channels together tell a complete story of how an external institutional innovation reshapes internal cost behavior."


## Slide 7：谁更受益 — 异质性（1 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 分组对比图（左右对比每组系数）：高 vs 低信息不对称 | 非国企 vs 国企 | 乐观 vs 非乐观管理者
- 显著组的系数标亮

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "Not all firms benefit equally. I tested three dimensions of heterogeneity."
>
> "First, **information asymmetry**. The effect is highly significant for firms with high information asymmetry — where analyst coverage is low — but insignificant for firms with low asymmetry. **This makes sense: the policy's main benefit is improving information transparency, so firms that start with worse information environments gain more.** "
>
> "Second, **ownership type**. The effect is significant for non-state-owned enterprises but not for SOEs. SOEs already have stronger government backing and softer budget constraints, so the marginal benefit of the policy is smaller."
>
> "Third, **managerial optimism**. Firms with overconfident managers — measured by their tendency to hold options — show a stronger effect. Digital tools help correct the optimistic bias that would otherwise lead managers to delay necessary cost cuts."
>
> "These heterogeneity results **not only deepen our understanding** of the policy, but also provide guidance for targeted policy design."


## Slide 8：稳健性检验（1 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 一个雷达图或表格摘要，列出5种方法的结果一致性
- 不用展开讲数据，突出"all consistent"

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "I performed **five sets of robustness checks** to make sure my results are solid."
>
> "First, **replacing the dependent variable** — using administrative expenses alone instead of total SG&A. Second, a **placebo test** — randomly assigning the policy shock and running 500 permutations, confirming the real result is not driven by chance. Third, **fixed effects model** controlling for city-level effects. Fourth, **PSM-DID** — using propensity score matching to address selection bias. And fifth, **excluding confounding policies** — controlling for the effects of other concurrent policies like the Innovative City Pilot, Broadband China strategy, and others."
>
> "I won't go into each in detail. The key takeaway: **my core finding holds in every single robustness check.** This gives me confidence that the result is not an artifact of model choice or confounding factors."


## Slide 9：结论 + 我的收获与反思（1.5 min）

**视觉设计建议：**
- 左侧：三点总结
- 右侧：两列 — "What I Found" / "What I Learned"
- 最后放一句有力的结束语

**英文讲稿全文：**

> "Let me conclude with **what I found and what I learned from this process.** "
>
> "**What I found:** The Digital Economy Innovation Development Pilot Zones significantly reduce corporate cost stickiness. This works through three mechanisms — upgrading labor structure, increasing R&D investment, and strengthening internal controls. The effect is stronger for non-SOEs, firms with high information asymmetry, and firms with overconfident managers."
>
> "**What I learned:** "
>
> "First, **how to design a rigorous empirical study** — from identifying a research gap, to choosing an identification strategy, to cleaning data, running regressions, and testing robustness. This process taught me that good research is not about finding significant results — it's about asking the right question and eliminating alternative explanations."
>
> "Second, **that research questions can come from personal experience.** My grandfather's story is not just an emotional hook — it reflects a real economic problem that deserves academic attention."
>
> "Third, **the value of persistence.** There were times when results weren't clean, when robustness checks failed, when the story didn't make sense. Working through those challenges taught me more than any textbook could."
>
> "I recognize this study has limitations — the sample covers only listed firms, the policy has been in place for only about five years, and I cannot fully rule out all confounding factors. **But I believe this thesis makes a meaningful first step** in connecting macroeconomic policy to micro-level cost behavior."
>
> "I look forward to exploring this line of research further in my future career."
>
> "Thank you. I welcome your questions."
